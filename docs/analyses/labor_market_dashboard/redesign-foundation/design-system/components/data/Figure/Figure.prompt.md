Wrap EVERY chart in Figure — it supplies the eyebrow, the finding-first title, the as-of stamp, and the source/denominator note that the house style requires.

    <Figure eyebrow="Year over year" title="Growth and decline in postings, year over year" asOf="Jul 2026" note="Each bar compares a month with the same month a year earlier.">
      {chart}
    </Figure>

Titles state the reading ("Is growth broad or narrow?"), never the chart type. Notes carry denominators and caveats.
